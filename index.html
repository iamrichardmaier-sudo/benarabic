Why roots?

Arabic builds vocabulary from roots. كَتَبَ means "he wrote"; the same three letters give كِتَاب "book", كَاتِب "writer" and مَكْتَبَة "library". Learn the root and the pattern, and a new word is half-learned before you meet it. How the root system works →
