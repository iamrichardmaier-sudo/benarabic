ق-د-م

The Arabic root ق-د-م (q-d-m)

Core sense: coming forward, advancing; precedence; the foot

12 words in this corpus are built from ق-د-م, spanning verb forms II, V. In Arabic, a root is a skeleton of consonants; slotting it into different patterns (awzān) produces related words with predictable shifts in meaning.
