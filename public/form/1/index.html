فَعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form I — The plain action

The base form. It carries the root idea with nothing added — whatever the root means at its simplest.

At a glance

Pattern: فَعَلَ
Does: The plain action
Attested here: 424 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form I is written فَعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
