Arabic Verb Form IX — Colours and defects

A narrow form, almost entirely used for becoming a colour or acquiring a bodily defect.

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form IX is written اِفعَلَّ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
