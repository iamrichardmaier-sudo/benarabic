اِفتَعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form VIII — Reflexive, often idiomatic

An infixed ت. Frequently the action done for oneself or to oneself, though many Form VIII verbs drift into their own meanings.

At a glance

Pattern: اِفتَعَلَ
Does: Reflexive, often idiomatic
Attested here: 99 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form VIII is written اِفتَعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
