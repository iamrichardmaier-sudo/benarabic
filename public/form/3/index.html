فاعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form III — Done to or with someone

Stretching the first vowel points the action at another party — you do it to them, or alongside them.

At a glance

Pattern: فاعَلَ
Does: Done to or with someone
Attested here: 43 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form III is written فاعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
