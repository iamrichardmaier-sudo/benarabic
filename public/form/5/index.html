تَفَعَّلَ

Arabic Verb Form V — Reflexive of Form II

Form II with a تَ in front: the action turns back on the doer, or is undergone gradually.

At a glance

Pattern: تَفَعَّلَ
Does: Reflexive of Form II
Attested here: 79 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form V is written تَفَعَّلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
