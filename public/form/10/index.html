اِستَفعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form X — Seeking or considering

The اِست prefix means asking for the root action, or regarding something as having that quality.

At a glance

Pattern: اِستَفعَلَ
Does: Seeking or considering
Attested here: 28 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form X is written اِستَفعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
