فَعَّلَ

Arabic Verb Form II — Causative or intensive

Doubling the middle letter either makes someone else do the action, or makes it thorough and repeated.

At a glance

Pattern: فَعَّلَ
Does: Causative or intensive
Attested here: 166 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form II is written فَعَّلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
