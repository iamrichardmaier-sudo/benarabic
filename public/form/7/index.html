اِنفَعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form VII — Passive or resultative

The subject undergoes the action, often with no agent named — it simply happens to them.

At a glance

Pattern: اِنفَعَلَ
Does: Passive or resultative
Attested here: 33 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form VII is written اِنفَعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
