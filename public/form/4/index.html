أَفعَلَ

Arabic Verb Form IV — Causative

A prefixed hamza makes something or someone undergo the root action. Often blunter and more transitive than Form II.

At a glance

Pattern: أَفعَلَ
Does: Causative
Attested here: 147 verbs

How to recognise it

Every Arabic verb form is described by weighing it against the template root ف-ع-ل. Form IV is written أَفعَلَ, so any root poured into that same shape behaves the same way. That template is the word's wazn — its measure.
