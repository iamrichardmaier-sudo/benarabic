The ten Arabic verb forms

Arabic verbs are derived by pouring a root into one of ten templates. Each template — each wazn — shifts the root's meaning in a predictable way: making it causative, reflexive, mutual, or something else. Learning the ten patterns means you can often guess a verb's meaning from a root you already know.
